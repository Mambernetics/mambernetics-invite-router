Links for Calendar Reminders

Someone let me know that using ICS calendar files may not work on Android devices without Outlook installed. Here are a couple of options:

  1. ICS files can be imported into Google Calendar, but as far as I know this would need to be done on a PC via Settings → Import/Export in Google Calendar.
  2. Another option is to use a Google Calendar event template URL. However, these do not support recurring events. The google links below will let you add these to your calendar.
